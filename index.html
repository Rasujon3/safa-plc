About Us

International standards. Bangladeshi spirit. Safa PLC is redefining luxury hospitality in Bangladesh.

Safa PLC is a next-generation premium hospitality developer, building world-class five-star hotels and resorts across Bangladesh’s top tourism destinations. From Cox’s Bazar to Sajek Valley, we combine international luxury with local culture, crafts, and nature’s beauty.

Our mission is clear: create landmark destinations that attract global travelers while promoting sustainable development, creating jobs, and strengthening the economy. We don’t just build properties — we build experiences that reflect the heart of Bangladesh.

Our Mission & Vision

Shaping the future of luxury hospitality in Bangladesh

Our Vision

To become Bangladesh’s most influential luxury hospitality developer — crafting iconic destinations that inspire travelers, empower communities, and set new global benchmarks in elegance, experience, and sustainability.

Our Mission

To shape the future of tourism through architectural excellence, world-class service innovation, sustainable development, and culturally rooted design — creating unforgettable environments that celebrate Bangladesh’s natural beauty and spirit.
